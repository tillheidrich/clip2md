2. Processing of pasted content

Converting formatted text into Markdown happens entirely client-side, by JavaScript in your browser. Content you paste never leaves your device and is discarded when you close or reload the page. To that extent, we do not process any personal data within the meaning of the GDPR.

Saving the result as a .md file and copying it to the clipboard also happen locally through the corresponding browser interfaces. No data is sent to us.

The “Paste” button reads your device's clipboard. This only happens when you press the button and, depending on the browser, only after you have given permission. As with pasting via the keyboard, the content is converted locally only. On smartphones, “Save” hands the file to your operating system's share sheet; where you pass it on from there is up to you.

Cleaning up pasted web pages and formatting Reddit threads also run entirely in your browser. The optional “Open in Obsidian” button places the text on the clipboard and hands it to the Obsidian app on your device; nothing is transmitted to us or third parties.

3. Local storage of your settings

So that the site remembers your preferences, it stores values in your browser's local storage (localStorage), such as your chosen language, light or dark appearance and your conversion settings. These values stay on your device, are not transmitted to us and contain none of the content you paste. They are not cookies. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings.

Only if you switch on “Keep the last 10 clips” in the settings does the site store the most recently edited texts in your browser's local database (IndexedDB). This feature is off by default. The entries never leave your device, are only visible in this browser and can be deleted individually or all at once in the history; when you switch the feature off, you are asked whether the history should be deleted.

The basis for this purely local storage is your wish for a working user interface; we have no access to it.

4. Offline use (Progressive Web App)

The site registers a service worker that stores the required program files in your browser's cache, so clip2md also works without an internet connection. Only program files of this site are cached, never your content. You can remove the cache via your browser's site data.

5. Server log files

When this site is requested, the hosting provider automatically stores data in log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • name and URL of the requested file
  • amount of data transferred and whether the request succeeded
  • browser and operating system used
  • where applicable, the previously visited page (referrer)

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the technically error-free operation and security of the service. The data is deleted after seven days at the latest and is not combined with other data sources.

7. No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or audience measurement services. No content is loaded from third-party servers, in particular no fonts and no scripts from content delivery networks. A consent banner is therefore not required.

One exception concerns the optional preview: if the text you paste contains images hosted on third-party servers, your browser loads those images from there when the preview is shown. The respective server learns your IP address in the process. None of your text is transmitted, and the origin is not sent thanks to Referrer-Policy: no-referrer. If you want to avoid this, keep the preview switched off.
